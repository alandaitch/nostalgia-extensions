Review instructions — Fotolog para Instagram 2.3.0

1. Install the extension, then open https://www.instagram.com/ with regular access to Instagram. The extension has no account system or backend and does not bypass Instagram authentication. No extension-specific credentials, payments or subscription are required.
2. On a loaded home feed, check the Fotolog index, Fotos/Reels filters, author links and the control to load more posts.
3. Open a post or an author profile. The diary has three columns. Selecting a reel thumbnail loads the site's original video and starts it muted. Check pause/play and enabling sound.
4. Open Colores in the diary. Choose a palette or Al azar por usuario; the same author retains a deterministic palette. Mezclar paletas changes that mapping.
5. Add a profile to favorites; it appears in the sidebar. Open another profile and verify the recent profiles list. Only up to 12 account names are kept in chrome.storage.session; favorites are in chrome.storage.local.
6. The toolbar popup changes the palette, diary tagline and enabled state. Disable the skin to restore Instagram. The original login, messages, stories, forms and other unsupported screens remain native.

All executable code is in the package. Photo/video resources retain Instagram's original providers. All processed page content stays local; there is no analytics endpoint or developer server.

Public privacy policy: https://alandaitch.github.io/nostalgia-extensions/privacy.html#fotolog
