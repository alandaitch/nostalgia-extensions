No extension account, backend, payment or extra credentials. Open instagram.com with regular Instagram access. The home feed becomes a Fotolog index. Open a post/profile: select photo or reel thumbnails (reels start muted; test pause and sound), change Colores, add Favorites and visit another profile to see Recents. Use the extension popup to disable the skin and restore Instagram. Login, messages and stories remain native. All processing is local; no remote code.
